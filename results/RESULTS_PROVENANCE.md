# Results Provenance & Verification Audit

This document establishes the origin, classification, and reproducibility status of all experimental results associated with **FedUA-Net**.

---

## 1. Classification Framework

Every experimental metric and table entry in this repository is classified under one of four provenance categories:

- **`VERIFIED_REPRODUCED` (Category A)**: Results generated by executing the corrected, synchronized codebase under the multi-seed protocol in this environment, backed by stored raw per-sample/per-batch prediction CSVs and machine-readable summary logs.
- **`EXISTING_EXPERIMENTAL` (Category B)**: Legacy empirical results generated from prior experimental sweeps (e.g., initial sample-weighted baseline runs) stored in repository archives.
- **`PAPER_REPORTED` (Category C)**: Canonical benchmark numbers reported in the authoritative manuscript (*IEEE Transactions on Medical Imaging* submission).
- **`UNVERIFIED` (Category D)**: Intermediate exploratory runs whose full parameter traces are not preserved.
